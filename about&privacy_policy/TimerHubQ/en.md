## Data sources

- Open-Meteo for World Clock location searches. Its data are licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- Timer Hub Q processes these data for display and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Open-Meteo.

- Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/licence

## Data stored on your device

- Timer Hub Q stores saved World Clock locations and time zones, alarms and alarm history, timers and timer history, stopwatch state and archived sessions, settings, and cached copies of this document in its private application storage.
- Timer names, folder names, stopwatch session titles, schedules, durations, laps, and related timestamps remain on your device and are not sent to any external server for processing.

## Location data

- Timer Hub Q does not request or access your device’s location.
- World Clock search is optional. When you search, the text you enter and the app’s selected language are sent to Open-Meteo to find matching locations.
- If you add a search result, Timer Hub Q saves its city name, country code, and time zone on your device. It does not save the result’s coordinates.

## External services

- The developer of Timer Hub Q does not operate a backend or receive Open-Meteo requests.
- Timer Hub Q sends World Clock search terms and the selected language over HTTPS to Open-Meteo.
- Network traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
- When this document is opened inside the app, it is downloaded over HTTPS from Timer Hub Q’s public GitHub repository.
- External services may log IP addresses, search terms, request times, or technical details according to their own policies. Open-Meteo states that troubleshooting logs are deleted after 90 days.

## Backup, retention, and deletion

- Depending on device and settings, Android, Google, or the device manufacturer may include all of Timer Hub Q’s locally stored data—including World Clock entries, alarms and alarm history, timers and timer history, stopwatch data and archived sessions, settings, and cached copies of this document—in cloud backups or device transfers. The developer does not receive or control these copies; instead, you can manage them in your device or account backup settings. Backups may remain after this app is uninstalled.
- Local data remains until replaced, removed through the app, cleared through Android settings, or deleted when the app is uninstalled.
