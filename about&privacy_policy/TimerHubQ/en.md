## Data stored on your device

- Timer Hub Q stores saved World Clock locations and time zones, alarms and alarm history, timers and timer history, stopwatch state and archived sessions, settings, and cached copies of this document in its private application storage.
- Timer names, folder names, stopwatch session titles, schedules, durations, laps, and related timestamps remain on your device and are not sent to any external server for processing.
- Rounded coordinates, sunrise, sunset, daylight duration, time zone, and fetch time are cached locally for daylight themes and offline use. This cache is excluded from app exports and Android backups or device transfers; Android may evict it. Clear the app’s cache or data, or uninstall the app, to remove it. This does not delete external providers’ logs.

## Location data

- Auto (Daylight) sends your approximate phone location to Open-Meteo to get sunrise and sunset times while the app is open. If location or daylight data is unavailable, it follows System.
- Location permission is optional; other themes work without it. Timer Hub Q requests only approximate foreground location, never precise or background location. Coordinates are rounded to 0.1 degrees before being sent to Open-Meteo or cached. With permission already granted, daylight data may also be preloaded while the theme picker is open. Revoke the app’s location permission in Android settings to stop location access.
- World Clock search is optional. When you search, the text you enter and the app’s selected language are sent to Open-Meteo to find matching locations.
- If you add a search result, Timer Hub Q saves its city name, country code, and time zone on your device. It does not save the result’s coordinates.

## External services

- The developer of Timer Hub Q does not operate a backend or receive Open-Meteo requests.
- Timer Hub Q sends World Clock search terms and the selected language over HTTPS to Open-Meteo.
- Network traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
- When this document is opened inside the app, it is downloaded over HTTPS from Timer Hub Q’s public GitHub repository.
- External services may log IP addresses, coordinates, search terms, request times, or technical details according to their own policies. Open-Meteo states that troubleshooting logs containing coordinates are deleted after 90 days.
- Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy

## Backup, retention, and deletion

- Depending on device and settings, Android, Google, or the device manufacturer may include Timer Hub Q’s locally stored data in cloud backups or device transfers. The developer does not receive or control these copies, instead the user can manage them in their device or account backup settings. The backups may remain after this app is uninstalled.
- Local data remains until replaced, removed through the app, cleared through Android settings, or deleted when the app is uninstalled.
